# Contracts and Renewals

All Northwind contracts include a service-level agreement (SLA). If a customer suffers a picking outage longer than 4 hours that is caused by Northwind hardware or firmware, the customer receives a service credit of 5% of that month's FleetOS fee. Outages of 4 hours or less earn no credit.

## Halden Logistics
Three-year contract, renews on 2026-11-30. Annual contract value USD 610,000.

## Sunpeak Grocers
Two-year contract, renews on 2027-03-01. Annual contract value USD 1,240,000.

## Kestrel Pharma
Three-year contract, renews on 2026-12-15. Annual contract value USD 210,000. Kestrel may opt out of automatic renewal by giving written notice at least 60 days before the renewal date.
