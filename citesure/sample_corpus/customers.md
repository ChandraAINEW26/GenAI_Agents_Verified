# Customer Accounts

## Halden Logistics
Halden Logistics is a third-party logistics provider based in Oslo, Norway. It operates 40 AX-200 arms and FleetOS at its Gardermoen distribution centre. The account manager is Ravi Menon.

## Sunpeak Grocers
Sunpeak Grocers is a grocery chain based in Austin, Texas. It runs 120 Courier C3 robots across three fulfilment centres, managed with FleetOS. The account manager is Priya Shah.

## Kestrel Pharma
Kestrel Pharma is a pharmaceutical distributor based in Basel, Switzerland. It uses 12 AX-200 arms in a temperature-controlled warehouse. The account manager is Ravi Menon.
