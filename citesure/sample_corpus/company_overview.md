# Northwind Robotics — Company Overview

Northwind Robotics is a fictional warehouse-automation company founded in 2016 and headquartered in Pune, India. It has regional offices in Rotterdam (serving Europe, Middle East and Africa) and Austin (serving the Americas).

The chief executive officer is Meera Iyer, who co-founded the company with chief technology officer Daniel Okafor. Northwind sells robotic hardware together with a fleet-management software subscription, and most revenue comes from multi-year contracts.

Northwind organises its commercial team by region. Each customer account has exactly one named account manager who owns renewals, expansion and escalations for that customer.
