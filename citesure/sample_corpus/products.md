# Product Catalog

## AX-200 Warehouse Arm
The AX-200 is a six-axis picking arm for tote and shelf picking. Its grip controller runs the AX firmware line. Firmware 4.2 was released in April 2026 and added faster grip calibration. Firmware 4.2.1, released on 2026-05-21, fixed a memory leak in the grip controller that could freeze the arm after roughly five hours of continuous operation. List price is USD 48,000 per arm.

## Courier C3 Delivery Robot
The Courier C3 is an autonomous cart that moves goods between picking zones and loading docks. It uses lithium iron phosphate battery packs sourced from an external cell supplier. List price is USD 21,500 per unit.

## FleetOS
FleetOS is the subscription software that schedules, monitors and updates every Northwind robot at a site. It pushes firmware updates to AX-200 arms and Courier C3 robots. FleetOS is priced at USD 90 per robot per month.
