## Reset a forgotten password
Open the FleetOS sign-in page and choose "Forgot password". Enter the email address on your FleetOS account. A reset link arrives within 5 minutes and stays valid for 30 minutes. If the link has expired, request a new one.

## Locked accounts
After 5 failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes. Wait 15 minutes, then reset the password. Your company's FleetOS administrator can also unlock the account immediately from Settings > Users.

## Single sign-on
If your company signs in with single sign-on (SSO), FleetOS cannot reset your password. Contact your own IT team.
