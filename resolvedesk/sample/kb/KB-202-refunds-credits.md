## Duplicate charges
If you were charged twice for the same invoice, support can refund the duplicate payment. Refunds up to USD 500 are approved by support and reach your account within 5 to 7 business days. Refunds above USD 500 must be approved by the billing team, which replies within 2 business days.

## SLA service credits
If a picking outage caused by Northwind hardware or firmware lasts longer than 4 hours, you receive a service credit of 5% of that month's FleetOS fee. Credits are applied automatically to the next invoice after the incident review is complete. Outages of 4 hours or less do not earn a credit.
