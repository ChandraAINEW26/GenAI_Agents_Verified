## Invoices
Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month and can be downloaded from Billing > Invoices. Payment terms are 30 days from the invoice date.

## Changing the payment method
Administrators can update the card or bank details in Billing > Payment methods. For security, never send card numbers to support by email or chat; support staff will never ask for them.
