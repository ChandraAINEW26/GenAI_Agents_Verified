## Adding users
FleetOS administrators can invite users from Settings > Users > Invite. Invitations expire after 7 days.

## Roles
There are three roles: Viewer (read-only dashboards), Operator (can pause and assign robots) and Administrator (users, billing and firmware). Only an Administrator can change another user's role.
