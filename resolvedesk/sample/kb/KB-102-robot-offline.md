title: A robot shows as offline in FleetOS
category: technical
---
## Quick checks
1. Check the robot's status light. Solid blue means it is powered and connected; blinking amber means it has no network.
2. Confirm the robot is within range of a site Wi-Fi access point. Courier C3 robots need a signal of at least -70 dBm.
3. Restart the robot by holding the power button for 10 seconds, then wait 3 minutes for it to reconnect.

## If the robot stays offline
In FleetOS, open Fleet > Robots, select the robot and choose "Run diagnostics". Send the diagnostics ID to support. If more than 3 robots at one site go offline at the same time, treat it as a site network outage and contact support by phone.
